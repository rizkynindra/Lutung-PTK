# LUTUNG PTK Design Direction

## Design read

Public-sector technology portal for general users, internal stakeholders, and developers.
Professional, direct, and recognizably Indonesian without decorative nationalism.

**Dial:** ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Identity

- Use a text wordmark until PTK supplies an approved logo.
- Use the Borussia Dortmund kit: black for every page and panel background, yellow (`#FDE100`)
  for reading text, and white for supporting text, borders, and secondary controls.
- Fill yellow only on the wordmark mark, primary actions, the demo warning, beta status, and
  table headers. Those surfaces use black text so the kit contrast stays readable.
- Use system sans-serif typography for dependable rendering on managed internal devices.
- Use a narrow woven-line motif sparingly in section dividers. It references Indonesian craft
  without competing with technical content.

## Layout and interaction

- Prioritize the working catalog and playground over decorative landing-page sections.
- Vary section composition only when content hierarchy requires it.
- Keep catalog cards structurally consistent because users compare status, capability, and actions
  across technologies; vary the surrounding section composition instead.
- Use rectangular controls with modest corners and visible keyboard focus.
- Motion is limited to state transitions and hover feedback.
- Every status, metric, and service claim must come from configuration or a real response.
